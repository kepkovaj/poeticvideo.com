*** "As of March 17th, 2009 Poetic Video's 'Stanislav Stanek My Blues And Greens' movie has been declared the undisputed winner of the BEIJING FILM FESTIVAL CRYING MONKEY AWARD for non-fiction film. A note on the origin of the Crying Monkey award: It was originally proposed by Hollywood Screenwriter Justin Theroux in his 2008 screenplay: 'Tropic Thunder'. Seeing as how art imitates life and life imitates art, we at the New Beijing International Movie Festival are the only Beijing Film Festival capable of bringing the Crying Monkey tradition to life and granting such an award!" Peter Sallade
